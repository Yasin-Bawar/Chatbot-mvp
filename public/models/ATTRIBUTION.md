- Source: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/ (STL files from the GitHub copy
  https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D)
- Changes: about 870 parts were merged into 27 organ and bone groups, moved to metres with Y up,
  simplified with meshoptimizer, quantized and meshopt-compressed by `scripts/build-body-model.mjs`.
  Colours were added for teaching.
